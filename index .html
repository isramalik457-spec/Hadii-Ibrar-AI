Strategy Overview

Instrument: XAUUSD · Higher timeframe: H4 · Entry timeframe: M5 · Maximum stop distance: $12.00 Gold price movement · Minimum RR: 1:2 · Preferred RR: 1:3

  • Double Top / Triple Top — SELL: At or around a relevant H4 level/zone, wait for a completed bearish M5 engulfing, pin bar, or rejection candle. A neckline break is NOT required.
  • Double Bottom / Triple Bottom — BUY: At or around a relevant H4 level/zone, wait for a completed bullish M5 engulfing, pin bar, or rejection candle. A neckline break is NOT required.
  • H4 High liquidity sweep — SELL: First completed M5 candle breaks and closes above H4 High; second completed M5 candle reverses and closes back below H4 High. Signal only after candle two closes.
  • H4 Low liquidity sweep — BUY: First completed M5 candle breaks and closes below H4 Low; second completed M5 candle reverses and closes back above H4 Low. Signal only after candle two closes.
  • OB / FVG retest: M5 retest of a relevant order block or untested OB/FVG at H4 context, followed by a valid completed M5 confirmation candle.
  • Scalping: Untested M5 OB/FVG at or near H4 High, Low, Open, or Close. Prefer compact-base OBs; entry requires a valid M5 confirmation. TP1 is the next relevant H4 candle high/low; TP2 is the next H4 level or opposite H4 level.
  • Stop loss: Beyond the confirmation candle, relevant zone, or sweep wick. Maximum distance is $12.00 in Gold price movement; there is no $10 minimum. Skip any setup needing more than $12.00.
  • Targets: TP1 and TP2 must each meet at least 1:2 risk/reward; preferred target is 1:3. Do not force a target if the required RR is unavailable.
  • H4 liquidity, OB, FVG, swing high/low and H4 OHLC context are calculated for analysis but are not drawn as chart levels.
  • Only completed candles and valid, fresh LIVE data may be used. If live data is missing, stale, or unverified, show no signal.
  • Signals are informational for manual review only. No automatic order execution.
